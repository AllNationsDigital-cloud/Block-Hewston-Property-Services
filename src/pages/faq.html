What don’t you cover?

Licensed plumbing, electrical and HVAC work. When a unit needs it, we refer you to licensed trades and can coordinate scheduling so it fits the turn. We also don’t take projects over $7,500, and we don’t handle hazardous materials.

Do you take on larger renovations?

No. Our work is scoped under $7,500 per project, which suits turnovers, cleanouts, paint and minor repairs. For bigger renovations we’ll tell you at the walkthrough and recommend the right contractor.
