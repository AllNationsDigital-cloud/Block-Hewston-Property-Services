How our property manager programs work

How we quote.

  • Starting rates shown above are for typical units in typical condition.
  • We walk the unit (or review photos) before pricing.
  • You receive a written, itemized quote. No work starts without your approval.
  • If we find additional scope mid-job, we quote it first. No surprise invoices.
Scope and limits. We take projects scoped under $7,500. Larger renovations, and any licensed plumbing, electrical or HVAC work, are referred to licensed contractors. We can coordinate the handoff so your timeline holds.
